WIZARD'S CHESS 3D - PHOTOREALISTIC STONE & MARBLE EDITION (OFFLINE PACK)
========================================================================

Everything needed to play offline is packed inside this folder:
  - index.html (Full 3D game, procedural marble & stone textures, Web Audio acoustics)
  - lib/three.min.js (Local 3D WebGL Engine)
  - lib/chess.min.js (Local Chess Rules Engine)

HOW TO PLAY OFFLINE:
  1. Extract this ZIP folder anywhere on your computer.
  2. Double-click 'index.html' (or 'PLAY-OFFLINE-WINDOWS.bat') to launch in any browser with zero internet required!
